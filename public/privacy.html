Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in to DISTRO (https://distrov2-teal.vercel.app) using Google or Facebook, we collect your basic profile information (name, email address, and account ID) solely to authenticate your workspace session. When you use the DISTRO Chrome extension, it reads your joined Facebook group names and IDs so you can organize and schedule posts.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use this information exclusively to:

  • Authenticate your account and automatically link your Chrome extension to your DISTRO workspace.
  • Display your joined Facebook groups inside your dashboard.
  • Publish posts that you explicitly schedule and approve.

3. Data Storage and Sharing

Your data is stored securely in our database and locally in your browser's Chrome storage. We do not sell, rent, or transfer your personal data or Facebook group data to third parties, data brokers, or advertisers.

4. Your Control and Deletion

You can disconnect your account, clear local extension storage, or request deletion of your workspace data at any time from your DISTRO account settings or by contacting us through the support email listed on our Chrome Web Store page.
